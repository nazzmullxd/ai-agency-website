Who is iZN for?

Companies that already serve business clients and want to add AI expertise without building a full internal team. This includes agencies, consultancies, technology partners and professional service businesses. Our initial market focus is the United Kingdom.

Who owns the client relationship?

You do. Your business manages the client relationship and presents the work under its own name. Any direct involvement or shared branding is agreed with you first.

Do we need a fully formed idea?

No. Start with the problem and what you know. The feasibility stage is where we explore the approach, data requirements, risks and whether AI is suitable.

How are projects priced?

Pricing depends on the scope, data and systems involved. We define the required work and estimated effort before a project commitment.

What happens after launch?

We can provide maintenance, improvements and support. The scope and support arrangement are agreed around each project.
