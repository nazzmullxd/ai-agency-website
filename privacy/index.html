How this website uses information when you explore a workflow or prepare a partnership brief.

Website visits

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts are served with the site. Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service.

Interactive document demo

The text you submit is sent to our server to extract labelled fields. Edited fields are sent back for validation when you approve the record. The application does not store these documents or records. Use fictional examples and do not submit confidential, sensitive or personal client information.

Partnership brief

Your name, email, company, service selection and message are processed by the server to prepare a brief and return it to your browser. The application does not save these details to a database, subscribe you to communications or send an email. You can download the prepared brief to your own device. Hosting infrastructure may retain technical request metadata; we do not intentionally log submitted form content.

Your controls

You can edit your details before preparing a brief, choose whether to download it, and reset the document demo. Reloading or leaving the page clears the working information from the page. Downloads remain on your device until you remove them.

Project data

Data access, hosting, permissions, retention and handling for a client project must be agreed separately before development or transfer of client data.
